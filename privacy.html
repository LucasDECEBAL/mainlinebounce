Privacy & Cookies

What we collect when you book, why we collect it, and how you can control it.

1. Who we are

Main Line Bounce is a family-run party rental business serving the Main Line area and the Greater Philadelphia region. You can reach us at (267) 405-3125.

2. Data we collect

When you place a booking we collect: your name, email address, phone number, event address and location, event date, number of guests and any comments you add. Payment card details are processed directly by Stripe and never reach our servers.

3. Why we use it

We use this information to confirm and organise your rental, to contact you about your event, to send you a receipt or reminder, and to keep a record of past bookings.

4. Cookies and advertising

We use a Google Ads tag to measure the performance of our advertising. This tag may set cookies. Nothing is stored for advertising or analytics until you press Accept in the cookie banner. If you press Decline, the tag runs in a restricted mode without ad or analytics storage.

You can change your choice at any time by clearing your browser's local storage for this site, which will make the banner appear again.

5. Sharing

We share data only with the services needed to run the business: Stripe (payments), our email provider (booking notifications) and Google Ads (only with your consent). We never sell your personal data.

6. Retention

Booking records are kept for as long as needed for accounting and customer-service purposes. You can ask us to delete your data at any time.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal data by calling (267) 405-3125. We will respond within a reasonable delay.
